# From Khipu research to auditable AI — acceptance roadmap

This design draws on the **documented** decimal accounting features of numerical khipus, the Open Khipu Repository and Khipu Field Guide; it does not reconstruct ancient cryptography or claim all khipus are numerical. Modern polynomial fingerprints, cryptographic digests, authority boundaries and machine learning are SZL's *new engineering choices*, not discoveries of ancient Inka computer science.

## Primary source-backed leaders

- OKR Advisory Board / OKR v2.1.0 (FitzPatrick, Brezine, Ghezzi, Hyland, Medrano and others): stable identifiers, archived records, explicit ethics, provenance and inclusive stewardship.
- Manuel Medrano and Ashok Khosla, *How Can Data Science Contribute to Understanding the Khipu Code?* (2024): transparent quantitative analysis and archival linkages.
- Khipu Field Guide's public corpus and Ascher summation studies: arithmetic structure with documentation of transcription and preservation limitations.
- Marcia and Robert Ascher: historical numerical relations and the scholarly origins of computer-assisted khipu arithmetic studies.
- Recent structural pattern mining (Contreras, 2026): supervised methods may also learn museum acquisition/recording confounders; demonstrate site-held-out validation rather than spurious high accuracy.
- Indigenous language/data sovereignty scholarship (Cushman et al., 2026): archive availability does not extinguish provenance, attribution, relationships or consent duties.

## Frontier hypotheses (not results)

1. **Uncertainty-aware cord graph learning:** represent each missing, torn or uncertain cord reading as a set/interval rather than an invented value; require exact arithmetic compatibility before model-based ranking.
2. **Cross-collection consistency with provenance:** graph edges must be admitted source relationships, not lexical or country-derived associations. A system should explain alternate independent support paths and what fails when evidence is removed.
3. **Digital khipu witnesses:** pair a user-declared arithmetic record with a source commitment, preserve a local replay receipt, and reject a model suggestion that disagrees with the deterministic check.
4. **Community-reviewed inference:** compare a non-ML arithmetic baseline, structural classifier, and null/permutation controls on material that stewards approve, with independent recorder/site-held-out sets, uncertainty and Indigenous researcher participation.

## Operational admission gates

- Stage A (this package): genuine tiny trained router + bounded synthetic dataset + source registry + working local interactive service. **PASSED only where recorded.**
- Stage B: source-on-GitHub PR, tests, DCO and exact SHA readback. Not established by local files alone.
- Stage C: legal, cultural and per-asset rights approval to include original archaeological records; an openly hosted ZIP is not blanket approval for model training.
- Stage D: separately approved 1.5B fine-tuning job with explicit budget/owner/locks, immutable model/tokenizer/dataset, checkpoint and optimizer preservation, independent heldout and negative controls.
- Stage E: Hugging Face dataset/model/Space publication with write authority, returned immutable commits, HF rendered readback, live deployment and rollback. Repo/source CI or HTTP 200 is not this gate.
- Stage F: peer-reviewed, multi-institution and community-backed study. No broad claims before independent review.

**Historical truth, software correctness, trained weights, evaluation and production are separate evidence categories.**
