# NOT_REDISTRIBUTABLE — rights-flagged material

The recovered Harvard KDB data layer offered to the OKR in khipulab/open-khipu-repository#1 contains material this project will not fetch, mirror, or train on until the OKR Advisory Board and the Khipu Database Project's rights position is settled:

1. **14 alternate assessments (2,323 cords)** of khipus the OKR holds under merged designations (e.g. AS070, a 590-cord assessment of KH0083, which the OKR holds under its UR035 assessment).
2. **AB001 = KH0701** (156 cords) — the OKR catalogs it (issued 2025-12-19) but holds no cord records.
3. **Urton khipu-type tables**, including ARCHIVE_NUM assignments used in the Urton & Brezine publications.

Basis: the Zenodo recovery record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21514490) states the recovered files are NOT republished there — they remain the intellectual work of the Harvard Khipu Database Project (Gary Urton, PI; Carrie Brezine, database manager) and were offered to the OKR maintainers. The recovery RECORD (manifest, tooling, validation) is open and IS ingested.

## Recorded ethics context (from the OKR#1 thread, A. Khosla, 2026-08-08)

- Urton-era recording shortcuts are documented: readings sometimes taken right-to-left instead of left-to-right; knot chirality inverse to common practice.
- The Khipu Field Guide data is regarded by senior field members as the corrected layer (4-6 person-years of corrections).
- Any eventual use of recovered KDB material must carry recording-vintage caveats, and alternate assessments must be treated as independent readings, not ground-truth.

When the OKR board decides (accept / publish separately / decline), this file and the corpus-v2 manifest get updated by signed PR in the same spirit. Until then, the only artifact ingested from this recovery is the open 32.1 kB record that lets any future holder verify content identity by checksum.
